# NHS CONTRACT — BACKGROUND IP & TRADE MARK CLAUSE (DRAFT FOR SOLICITOR REVIEW)
**Prepared for the NHS England engagement (1 Oct call context) · Draft v1.0 · 29 Aug 2026 · All IP belongs to JDB Sales.**

> **How to use:** propose this as the IP clause in the contract/SOW alongside the NHS T&Cs (Schedule 2, General T&Cs, clauses 11.1/11.2). It preserves YOUR ownership of background IP (platform, marks, code) while granting the NHS the licence it needs. Have a solicitor review before signing — this is a draft, not advice.

---

## CLAUSE 1 — DEFINITIONS
**"Background IP"** means all intellectual property owned by or licensed to the Supplier prior to the commencement of this Contract or developed independently of it, including without limitation: (a) the AEGIS AI Security Platform and all associated software, source code, documentation and know-how; (b) the trade marks **ZEUS, ZEUSTA, ZEUS AI, ZEUSAI and AEGIS** and all associated goodwill; (c) the Supplier's tooling, models, training data methodologies and security libraries.

**"Foreground IP"** means intellectual property created by the Supplier in the course of performing this Contract which did not exist before its commencement.

## CLAUSE 2 — SUPPLIER'S BACKGROUND IP
2.1 All Background IP shall **remain the sole and exclusive property of the Supplier** (and, where applicable, its licensors).
2.2 The Supplier **grants to the Authority a non-exclusive, non-transferable, royalty-free licence** to use the Background IP solely for the purpose of receiving and using the deliverables under this Contract.
2.3 The Authority shall not use, or permit the use of, the Background IP for any purpose other than as permitted under Clause 2.2, including without limitation in connection with any third-party product or service.
2.4 The Authority acknowledges that the trade marks are the property of the Supplier and shall not challenge, nor assist any third party in challenging, the validity or ownership of the trade marks; any use of the marks by the Authority shall be in accordance with the Supplier's brand guidelines.

## CLAUSE 3 — FOREGROUND IP
3.1 Foreground IP ownership shall be **agreed in writing per work package** at the time the work is commissioned, taking into account: (a) the origin of the IP; (b) the extent of NHS funding or input; (c) the strategic intent of the programme.
3.2 As a default for this Contract: Foreground IP that is (i) a **modification, enhancement or configuration of the Supplier's Background IP** shall be owned by the **Supplier**, with the Authority receiving a licence under Clause 2.2 terms; (ii) genuinely new and separately identifiable software or documentation created specifically for the Authority may, by agreement and for value, be assigned to the Authority, with the Supplier retaining a licence back for its own business use.
3.3 Nothing in this Contract transfers or diminishes the Supplier's ownership of Background IP.

## CLAUSE 4 — NHS DATA
4.1 All NHS/national data processed under this Contract remains owned/controlled by the Authority. The Supplier processes it only as a processor for the Authority in accordance with the DPA, UK GDPR and the Contract's data-processing terms.

## CLAUSE 5 — TRADE MARKS & USE OF NAMES
5.1 The Authority may refer to the Supplier and its products by name in connection with the Contract, but shall not register, or attempt to register, any trade mark identical or confusingly similar to the Supplier's marks, nor use them other than in accordance with Clause 2.4.
5.2 Any public-facing materials referencing the Supplier's marks shall be approved by the Supplier before publication (brand-safety gate, consistent with the Supplier's governance policy `pol_marketing_publish`).

## CLAUSE 6 — ESCROW
6.1 The Supplier shall maintain the software source code in escrow in accordance with the source-code escrow agreement (referenced in the MSA), release conditions limited to insolvency events of the Supplier and the continuing need for the Authority to keep the contracted service operational.

## CLAUSE 7 — WARRANTIES
7.1 The Supplier warrants it owns or validly licenses all Background IP provided under this Contract, and that its use does not infringe third-party rights (supported by the trade-mark registrations filed in 2026).
7.2 The trade-mark registrations for ZEUS, ZEUSTA, ZEUS AI, ZEUSAI and AEGIS serve as verifiable evidence of the Supplier's ownership of its brand.
