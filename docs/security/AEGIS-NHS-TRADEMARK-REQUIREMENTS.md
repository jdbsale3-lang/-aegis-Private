# NHS TRADEMARK & IP REQUIREMENTS — RESEARCH BRIEF
**29 Aug 2026 · Sources: GOV.UK IP guidance for NHS England, NHS England Terms & Conditions for Supply · All IP belongs to JDB Sales.**

## 1. DOES THE NHS *REQUIRE* YOU TO OWN TRADEMARKS?
**No formal requirement to register trademarks to bid for or win NHS contracts.** No NHS tender, framework (e.g., NHS SBS, Atamis), or the NHS T&Cs demands pre-existing registered marks. What the NHS DOES require is **clarity of IP ownership** — that you demonstrably own, or are licensed to use, every piece of IP you supply. Registered trademarks are the strongest, cheapest proof of that ownership.

## 2. WHY IT MATTERS — THE NHS CONTRACT CLAUSES THAT CHANGE EVERYTHING
The **NHS Terms and Conditions for the Supply of Goods and the Provision of Services** (Schedule 2, General T&Cs) contains **Clauses 11.1 and 11.2**, which govern IP between supplier and the NHS Authority. Summary of the standard position:

- **Background IP (what you bring):** normally stays owned by you — the NHS takes a **licence to use** it for the contract purpose. This is why *pre-existing, registered* marks matter: a trademark registered before any NHS contract is clearly **background IP**.
- **Foreground IP (newly created during the contract):** the NHS standard terms lean toward the **NHS owning (or taking broad licences to) newly developed IP** — with wording that can extend that grant to any supplier/third party engaged by the Authority.
- **Risk:** if your mark/software is created *during* an NHS engagement or is entangled with their improvements, ownership can be contested. Registering first = clean background-IP status.

**Practical translation:** file the trademarks BEFORE any contract negotiation. It converts ZEUS/AEGIS/ZEUSTA into provable background IP that the NHS must licence rather than own.

## 3. WHAT THE NHS ACTUALLY CHECKS AT PROCUREMENT
| Check | Relevance to trademarks |
|---|---|
| Supplier declaration of IP ownership | Yes — you must confirm you own/control all supplied IP |
| Freedom to operate | Due diligence on your freedom to use your platform |
| DTAC / DSPT / clinical safety | Operational compliance — not trademark-related, but part of the same gate |
| Framework registration (Atamis/NHS SBS) | No trademark requirement |
| Cyber Essentials (mandatory for many NHS contracts) | No trademark requirement |
